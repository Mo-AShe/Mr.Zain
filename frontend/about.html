Our Story

Built in Assiut. Cut with quiet permanence.

MR. ZAIN began with a straightforward frustration: Egyptian winter streetwear was either overly loud or lacked fabric weight. We set out to craft understated garments that feel substantial the moment you put them on.

Brand Philosophy

Less noise, heavier cotton.

Every hoodie, sweatshirt, and jacket starts with raw material discipline. We prioritize dense brushed Egyptian fleece, tonal stitching, and proportions tailored for layering.

Rather than chasing weekly micro-trends, MR. ZAIN releases focused winter capsules designed to stay relevant season after season.
